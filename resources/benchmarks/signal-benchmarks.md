# Signal Benchmarks

Performance data and timing windows for buying signals.
Use these to prioritize outreach and set realistic expectations.

## Baseline Reply Rates

| Target | Reply Rate | Context |
|--------|------------|---------|
| Cold (No Signal) | 1-3% | Spray and pray outbound |
| ICP Matched (No Signal) | 4-6% | Good list, no timing |

## Lift by Signal Type

| Signal | Reply Rate | Lift Multiplier | Confidence |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|------------|
| Champion Job Change | 15-20% | 3-4x | High |
| 1st-Party Intent (Pricing Page) | 25-30% | 5x | High |
| 3rd-Party Intent (G2/TrustRadius)| 10-12% | 2x | Medium |
| Funding Announcement | 12-15% | 2.5x | Medium |
| Hiring a Specific Role | 8-10% | 2x | Low (Lagging indicator) |
| Tech Stack Change | 8-12% | 2x | Medium |

## Multi-Signal Stacking

| Stacking Depth | Average Reply Rate | Action |
|----------------|--------------------|--------|
| 1 Signal | 10-15% | Automated/SDR sequence |
| 2 Signals | 20-25% | Personalized SDR outreach |
| 3+ Signals | 35-40%+ | Immediate AE intervention |

## Optimal Timing Windows

When to strike for maximum conversion probability.

| Signal | Strike Zone | Why It Works Then |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| Job Change | Days 14-45 | They are settled but still evaluating infrastructure |
| Funding | Weeks 2-4 | Strategy is set, budgets are unlocking |
| Website Visit | < 24 Hours | Topic is actively top-of-mind |
| Hiring | Within 14 days | Pain is acute *before* the hire starts |
| Tech Install | Days 30-60 | Implementation friction is highest |

## Signal Decay

Signal value decays rapidly outside the expected window.

| Time Elapsed | Value Retention |
|--------------|-----------------|
| In Window | 100% |
| +30 Days | 50% |
| +60 Days | 25% |
| +90 Days | 0-10% (Essentially cold) |
