# Signal Detection Tools

Standard tools used to identify and capture buying signals for outbound action.

## 1st-Party Intent (Website Visitors)

Identifying anonymous traffic on your own properties.

| Tool | Cost Tier | Best For | Integration |
|------|-----------|----------|-------------|
| RB2B | Free/Low | Slack alerts, person-level identification (US only) | Push to Slack/n8n |
| Clearbit Reveal | High | Enterprise IP-to-company resolution | CRM enrichment |
| 6sense | Enterprise | Account-level intent scoring and ad targeting | CRM sync |
| Factors.ai | Medium | B2B website analytics and deanonymization | CRM/Slack |

## 3rd-Party Intent (Topic/Review Sites)

Identifying research happening off your website.

| Tool | Focus | Signal Type | Setup |
|------|-------|-------------|-------|
| G2 Buyer Intent | Review platform | High intent (comparing vendors) | Native CRM sync |
| Bombora | Content consumption | Topic surges across B2B web | CRM/Platform overlay|
| ZoomInfo Intent | broad web | Keyword/topic research | Native platform |

## Job Changes (Champion Tracking)

Tracking past customers or target personas moving to new companies.

| Tool | Reliability | Best Feature | Notes |
|------|-------------|--------------|-------|
| UserGems | Very High | Direct CRM integration, finds new emails | Expensive but high ROI |
| Champify | High | Focuses heavily on the champion play | Good alternative to UserGems |
| LinkedIn Sales Nav | Medium | "Changed jobs in past 90 days" filter | Manual process via lists |
| Clay | High | Automated LinkedIn URL checks | Best for custom workflows |

## Hiring & Growth Signals

Tracking headcount growth, open roles, and department expansions.

| Tool | Best Feature | Update Frequency |
|------|--------------|------------------|
| Apollo | Keyword search in job posts | Daily |
| PredictLeads | Specific hiring categories | Weekly |
| Clay | Native job board scraping | On-demand |
| LinkedIn Premium | Company Insights tab | Real-time |

## Firmographic Triggers & Funding

Tracking capital raises, acquisitions, and news.

| Tool | Data Quality | Good For |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Crunchbase | High | Series A-D funding, M&A |
| PitchBook | Very High | Private equity, venture data (Enterprise cost) |
| Apollo | Medium | General funding rounds |
| Owler | Low | Competitor news and basic triggers |

## Technographic (Stack Changes)

Tracking software installations and removals.

| Tool | Accuracy | Best For |
|------|----------|----------|
| BuiltWith | High | Web technologies, scripts, pixels |
| Wappalyzer | Medium | Browser-based discovery |
| HG Insights | Very High | Backend/infrastructure technologies |
| Slintel (6sense) | High | Contract renewal timing estimates |
