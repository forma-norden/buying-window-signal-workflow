# Champion Job Change

> **What it targets:** Former customers, champions, or serious late-stage prospects who move into a new target account.

## The logic

This is one of the cleanest timing signals because context already exists.

You are not creating awareness from zero. You are following a person who already knows the category, the problem, or your company. That does not guarantee a deal. It does justify fast qualification.

## SOP

> **Step 1: confirm the move**
>
> - Check title, company, and start date
> - Confirm the new company is still in ICP
> - Check whether the person was a customer, evaluator, or internal advocate
>
> **Step 2: enrich the new account**
>
> - Pull company domain
> - Pull current title and team structure
> - Identify one additional buying-committee contact
> - Verify work email
>
> **Step 3: route it**
>
> - Strong fit -> triggered outbound + AE follow-up
> - Medium fit -> LinkedIn warming + monitoring
> - Weak fit -> monitor only

## Message angle

Do not write:

> "Saw you started a new role."

Write to the operational change:

> "Looks like you're settling into a new revenue environment at {{company}}. That usually means stack scrutiny, process resets, and pressure to show movement fast."

## Short template

> Subject: new role at {{company}}
>
> {{first_name}} - looks like you've stepped into a new environment at {{company}}.
>
> New roles usually trigger a fast review of process, tooling, and where pipeline friction is hiding.
>
> If that is on your side of the table right now, I can send over the framework we use to map those gaps before teams add more headcount.

## Common mistake

Teams detect the move but send without updating the CRM, verifying the account, or attaching prior context.

That wastes the strongest part of the signal.
