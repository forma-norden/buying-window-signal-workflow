# Intent-Based Hiring Signals

> **What it targets:** Companies hiring for roles that imply budget, process pressure, or a manual workflow your system can replace or compress.

## The logic

Hiring is not just a headcount clue.

It is a budget and workflow clue.

If a company is hiring into a function your offer replaces, accelerates, or de-risks, that signal deserves qualification. It becomes stronger when combined with account fit and one supporting signal.

## SOP

> **Step 1: define the hiring pattern**
>
> Examples:
>
> - SDR / outbound hiring
> - RevOps hiring
> - demand generation hiring
> - multiple hires in one operational function
>
> **Step 2: qualify the account**
>
> - Is the account still in ICP?
> - Is the role connected to our problem space?
> - Is this one role or a broader team build?
>
> **Step 3: route by strength**
>
> - High-fit + strong role relevance -> outbound
> - High-fit + weak role relevance -> monitor
> - Weak-fit -> ignore

## Message angle

Do not position the note as "you should not hire."

Position it around operational leverage:

> "Most teams try to solve this with more headcount first and system design second. That is where ramp time and process drag usually pile up."

## Short template

> Subject: {{company}} + {{hiring_role}}
>
> {{first_name}} - saw the {{hiring_role}} hiring push at {{company}}.
>
> That usually signals one of two things: more capacity is needed, or the current process is too manual to scale cleanly.
>
> If the second problem is sitting behind the first one, I can send the framework we use to diagnose it before teams add more overhead.

## Common mistake

Forcing a pain narrative from the wrong role.

An SDR hire, a RevOps hire, and a VP Marketing hire do not mean the same thing. The role determines the angle.
