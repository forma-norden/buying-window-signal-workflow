### Why this library exists

Most B2B SaaS teams do not lose on signals because the concept is weak.

They lose because the signal reaches a bad system:

- broken CRM data
- weak enrichment coverage
- slow routing
- generic copy
- no clear decision on whether to send, warm, monitor, or escalate

The average cold email campaign still replies at **3.43%**, while the top 10% clear **10.7%+** (Instantly.ai, 2026). That gap is not just a copy gap. It is a timing and systems gap.

This library is the compact operating layer behind that difference.
