# Use This Filter Before You Trigger Outreach

> **Best for:** any signal that might trigger outbound, ABM, LinkedIn warming, or monitoring.

## Why this matters

Most teams do not have a signal problem.

They have a trigger-discipline problem.

This page exists to stop interesting clues from being treated like live buying windows.

## Read it fast

| If the score is... | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| `0-3` | Ignore or monitor |
| `4-6` | Enrich first, then re-score |
| `7-8` | Trigger a light motion |
| `9-10` | Move now |

## Use this scorecard

| Filter | Question | Score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICP fit | Is the account actually inside our target vertical, size, stage, and deal motion? | 0-3 |
| Timing strength | Does the event suggest active evaluation, budget movement, or operational change? | 0-3 |
| Contactability | Can we identify and reach the right role with verified data? | 0-2 |
| Route clarity | Do we know whether this belongs in outbound, ABM, LinkedIn, or monitoring? | 0-2 |

## The 4 checks that matter

1. Did something actually change?
2. Is the account still in our lane?
3. Can we reach the right person fast?
4. Do we know the correct first motion?

If any answer is no, do not send yet.

## Match score to route

| Score | Default route | What to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `0-3` | Ignore or monitor | Sending just because the signal sounds interesting |
| `4-6` | Enrich and watch | Writing copy before the data and owner are clear |
| `7-8` | Triggered outbound or LinkedIn | Escalating into full ABM too early |
| `9-10` | Full triggered motion | Waiting days and losing the timing edge |

## How teams waste it

A pricing-page revisit from a perfect-fit account with verified contacts is not the same as a loose hiring clue from a weak-fit company.

This filter exists so those two things do not get treated as equal.
