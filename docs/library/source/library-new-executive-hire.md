# New Executive Hire

> **What it targets:** New VP, CRO, Head of RevOps, Head of Demand Gen, or similar leadership hires inside a target account.

## The logic

New executives are one of the clearest signs that process, tools, and priorities may get re-evaluated.

The hire itself is not enough. The account still has to fit, and the motion still has to be routed correctly. But it gives you a legitimate "why now."

## Tool options

| Tool | Purpose | Alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sales Navigator [prospecting] | Track new role changes | Manual LinkedIn monitoring |
| Clay [enrichment / automation] | Pull role, company, and team context | Apollo.io [enrichment / prospecting] |
| HubSpot [CRM] | Assign owner and score signal | Salesforce [CRM] |
| Instantly.ai [sequencing] | Send triggered email motion | Smartlead [sequencing] |
| LinkedIn | Warm the account socially | Founder account manual touches |

## SOP

> **Step 1: validate the hire**
>
> - Confirm the role is inside the buying committee
> - Capture start date
> - Check whether the account is already being worked
>
> **Step 2: add context**
>
> - Current team size
> - Existing stack where visible
> - Open roles around the same function
> - Recent first-party behavior if available
>
> **Step 3: choose the route**
>
> - High-fit + supporting signals -> outbound now
> - High-fit only -> warm on LinkedIn first
> - Weak-fit -> monitor

## Why LinkedIn matters here

LinkedIn should support the motion, not replace it.

**68%** of B2B first-touch marketing interactions now originate on LinkedIn, and average DM response is around **10.3%**, though software and SaaS are lower at **4.77%** (MarketingLTB, 2026; CloselyHQ, January 2026; Belkins, 2026). The practical takeaway: let the executive see your point of view before or alongside the first email.

## Short template

> Subject: {{company}} leadership shift
>
> {{first_name}} - looks like you've just stepped into {{company}} at a point where most teams are reviewing process, tooling, and pipeline friction.
>
> If GTM systems are on your list right now, I can send the scorecard we use to identify where timing, routing, and data quality are slowing revenue teams down.

## Common mistake

Treating every new exec hire like an open buying project.

Use it as a route signal. Not a blanket excuse to pitch.
