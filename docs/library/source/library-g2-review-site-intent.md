# G2 / Review-Site Intent

> **What it targets:** Accounts actively researching your category through review sites, comparison pages, or other intent sources.

## The logic

This is weaker than first-party pricing-page behavior, but stronger than static list building.

It usually means the account is validating vendors, comparing options, or moving from general awareness into shortlist behavior. The signal is useful. It still needs qualification.

## Tool options

| Tool | Purpose | Alternative |

| G2 [intent] | Review-site research signal | Category comparison monitoring |

| HubSpot [CRM] | Route by account tier | Salesforce [CRM] |

## SOP

> **Step 1: confirm the account**
>
> - Match the account to your ICP
> - Check whether it is already in a target list
> - Check for supporting first-party activity
>
> **Step 2: enrich before action**
>
> - Pull 2-3 likely committee contacts
> - Add firmographic and stack context
> - Check whether the account is already in sequence or ad audiences
>
> **Step 3: choose the motion**
>
> - Strategic account -> ABM + outbound
> - Strong-fit account with clean contacts -> outbound
> - Weak supporting data -> monitor

## Route rule

Use this signal to prioritize and time outreach.

Do not use it to pretend you know the exact buying project.

## Short template

> Subject: {{company}} category research
>
> {{first_name}} - looks like your team may be actively evaluating this category right now.
>
> When that happens, most teams do not struggle with awareness. They struggle with timing, routing, and deciding which accounts actually deserve focused GTM effort.
>
> If useful, I can send the scorecard we use to separate active buying windows from noise.

## Common mistake

Writing overconfident copy from thin intent data.

Intent should improve prioritization. It should not make your outreach sound like guesswork dressed up as certainty.
